Bosch Dishwasher — Error Code E24

The drain pump is blocked or faulty.

brand Bosch appliance Dishwasher market US component drain pump category drainage severity stop failure

Ranked repair steps

1. Clean the drain filter

Open the door, remove the bottom rack, twist out the filter assembly, rinse away debris, reinstall, and run a test drain.

easy · basis: cost ascending

2. Clear the drain hose

Cut power, pull the unit out, remove kinks and blockages from the drain hose (flushing it if needed), and reattach with a proper high loop.

moderate · basis: cost ascending

3. Service the drain pump

Disconnect power and water, open the access panels, clear the pump impeller and housing, and replace the pump if it fails to run.

professional only · basis: cost ascending · parts $47.57–$117.00 (2026)
