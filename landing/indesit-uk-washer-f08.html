Indesit Washer — Error Code F08 (UK)

The heater relay cannot activate or is stuck; heater and pressure-switch connections are implicated.

brand Indesit appliance Washer market UK component heating relay category heating severity stop failure

ApplianceDB ranks 2 repairs for this code, led by “Reset and recheck”. That top fix is an easy DIY job, involving the heating relay. Severity is classified as stop failure — the appliance halts until the fault clears.

Ranked repair steps

1. Reset and recheck

Unplug for two to five minutes and restart a short cycle.

easy · basis: cost ascending

2. Service the heating circuit

Have the heating element and NTC tested for continuity, the heater-relay section of the PCB inspected for burn marks, and the pressure-switch wiring checked.

professional only · basis: cost ascending
