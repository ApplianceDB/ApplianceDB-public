Hoover Washer — Error Code E01 (UK)

The door lock (or drum-brake safety device on older collector-motor models) is not engaging correctly.

brand Hoover appliance Washer market UK component door interlock category door_lock severity stop failure

Ranked repair steps

1. Check the door and reset

Close the door firmly until it clicks, clear debris from the seal, and disconnect power for five to ten minutes before retrying.

easy · basis: cost ascending

2. Replace the door lock

If the error persists, have the interlock tested for continuity during the lock command and the door lock assembly replaced; check the control board if it continues.

professional only · basis: cost ascending
