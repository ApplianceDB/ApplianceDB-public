LG Washer — Error Code FE

Water rose past the safe threshold, indicating an overfill.

brand LG appliance Washer market US component water inlet valve category water_supply severity stop failure

ApplianceDB ranks 1 repair for this code, led by “Drain the overfill”. That top fix is a moderate-difficulty DIY job, involving the water inlet valve. Severity is classified as stop failure — the appliance halts until the fault clears.

Ranked repair steps

1. Drain the overfill

Close both water taps, power on, select the no-spin option, press Start/Pause to drain, then restart.
