Samsung Washer — Error Code DC

The door is not closed and locked.

Ranked repair steps

1. Secure the door

Confirm the latch is intact, clear anything caught in the door, and close it firmly until it locks.

2. Replace the door lock

If the latch is intact and the door still reads open, disconnect power and replace the door lock switch assembly.
