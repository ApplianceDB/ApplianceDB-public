Whirlpool Washer — Error Code HC

The hot and cold inlet hoses are connected in reverse.

brand Whirlpool appliance Washer market US component inlet hoses category water_supply severity degraded

ApplianceDB ranks 1 repair for this code, led by “Swap the hoses”. That top fix is an easy DIY job, involving the inlet hoses. Severity is classified as degraded — the appliance keeps running but not correctly.

Ranked repair steps

1. Swap the hoses

Turn off the water supply, reverse the hot and cold inlet hoses, and run a cycle to confirm.
