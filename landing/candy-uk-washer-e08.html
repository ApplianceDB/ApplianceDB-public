Candy Washer — Error Code E08 (UK)

The motor speed sensor is not reading drum speed correctly.

brand Candy appliance Washer market UK component tachogenerator category motor severity degraded

Ranked repair steps

1. Reset and lighten the load

Disconnect power for five to fifteen minutes, reduce the load, and try a short spin cycle with the door fully latched.

easy · basis: cost ascending

2. Service the motor and tachometer

If the speed fault returns, have the tachometer output measured, worn carbon brushes replaced, and the motor windings and drive triac checked.

professional only · basis: cost ascending
