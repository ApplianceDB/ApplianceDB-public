Pasos de reparación priorizados

1. Manually defrost the freezer

Unplug, remove food, and leave the doors open for at least eight hours; dry the interior, restart, and allow 24-48 hours to stabilize. Keep vents clear and doors aligned.

moderate · criterio: manufacturer first

2. Replace the evaporator fan motor

If the fan error returns after a full manual defrost, disconnect power and replace the evaporator fan motor.
