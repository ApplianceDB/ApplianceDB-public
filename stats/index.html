Error codes per brand

Samsung has the most verified codes in the corpus with 84 (19.2%), the only brand covered in both markets, followed by LG (73) and Whirlpool (41). 293 codes (66.9%) are US-market and 145 (33.1%) UK-market; 54.6% of all codes were derived from a manufacturer listing, the rest from a repair-reference listing.

Error codes per appliance type

Washers account for 57.1% of the corpus (250 codes), followed by dishwashers (73, 16.7%) and oven / ranges (45, 10.3%). The 26 brand-market-type pairs range from 27 codes (Miele UK washer) down to 10, the inclusion floor.

The most common fault categories

The largest fault category is sensor, 62 of 438 codes (14.2%), followed by water supply (48, 11.0%) and motor (45, 10.3%). The leading category differs by appliance: washers motor (36 of 250); dishwashers drainage / water supply (12 of 73); oven / ranges sensor (13 of 45); dryers ventilation (8 of 40); refrigerators fan / sensor (6 of 30).

How many codes stop the appliance

Of the 410 codes with a severity class, 32.7% are stop failure (the appliance halts until the fault clears), 60.0% degraded and 7.3% informational. 28 codes carry no severity and are excluded. Among categories with at least 10 classified codes, drainage has the highest stop-failure share (84.4%) and sensor the lowest (0.0%).

Which components the codes point to

The control board is the component most often named, by 46 codes (10.5%), ahead of the drain pump (28) and the water inlet valve (28). 147 distinct components are named across the corpus.

How much of the repair work is DIY

247 of 438 codes (56.4%) carry at least one ranked repair procedure, 288 in all. 50.3% of procedures are easy and 23.6% professional only; looking at the first-ranked procedure per code, 58.7% are easy and 19.0% professional only. 31 procedures consist of requesting professional service. The other 191 codes (140 washer, 51 dishwasher) come from sources that state no repair and carry none.

Method, reuse and citation

  • Source. The full ApplianceDB corpus retrieved 2026-07-16: 438 codes and 288 procedures, each re-derived from one of 30 fetched manufacturer support pages or disclosed repair references, never from model memory; meanings and steps are original paraphrase. Every row keeps its source tier and URL; see Sources and the data dictionary.
  • Nothing is padded. Codes whose source states no meaning were dropped, brand pairs under 10 verified codes were dropped, codes whose source lists no repair carry none, and codes without a severity class are excluded from the severity shares. Each section states its denominator.
  • Left out on purpose. Parts prices (17 priced OEM parts, 20 procedures with a cost) and labor minutes (0 populated) are too few to summarise; they stay row-level fields in the dataset.
  • Refresh. ApplianceDB is curated; this page and its charts are regenerated with each corpus edition, so figures move. Cite the snapshot date.
